Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026.

What MusicFinder collects

MusicFinder has no accounts and we do not collect your name, email, contacts or location. The words you type in the search box are sent to music catalogue services to fetch results; we do not store them.

Advertising

The app and this website show ads from Google (AdMob and AdSense). Google and its partners may use cookies or your device's advertising ID to show and measure ads, including personalised ads. You can read how Google uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and change ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland) the app asks for your consent before showing ads.

Remove-ads purchases

If you pay to remove ads, the payment is handled by a payment provider and we receive your email address so we can send your code. We use it for that purpose only.

Children

MusicFinder is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
