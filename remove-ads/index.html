Remove ads forever

MusicFinder is free because of ads. If you would rather not see them, pay once and they are gone for good. No subscription.

Remove-ads codes are not on sale yet. They will be available on this page soon.

How it works

  1. Pay once using the button above.
  2. You get an email with your personal code and an unlock link.
  3. On the phone with MusicFinder installed, tap the link. Or open the app, tap Remove ads at the bottom, paste the code and tap Unlock.

Good to know

  • The code keeps working after app updates and if you reinstall. Keep the email.
  • It removes the banner and the full-screen ads inside the app. This website still shows ads.
  • Everything in the app works the same with or without ads.
