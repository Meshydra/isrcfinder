How to install the APK

  1. Tap the download button above on your Android phone and wait for the file to finish.
  2. Open MusicFinder.apk from the notification or your Downloads folder.
  3. The first time, Android says your browser is not allowed to install apps. Tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.

Why Android shows a warning

Android shows the same notice for every app installed from outside an app store. MusicFinder asks for one permission only: internet access, which it needs to search. You can check your download against the SHA-256 value above to make sure the file was not changed on the way.

Updating

When a new version is out, download it here and install it over the old one. Your settings and any remove-ads code stay in place.

Removing the app

Press and hold the MusicFinder icon and choose Uninstall, like any other app.
